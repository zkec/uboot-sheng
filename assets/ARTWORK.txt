Current boot-menu artwork:

Android: head-only vector from Simple Icons, rendered in Android green.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/android.svg
Simple Icons: CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md
Android robot artwork originates with Google (CC BY 3.0).

Linux: maintained Tux vector by Garrett LeSage, refined by IFo Hancroft.
Original Tux illustration by Larry Ewing, made with The GIMP.
https://github.com/garrett/Tux (CC0)
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/garrett/Tux/master/tux.svg

Flat-color derivative: tools/flatten_tux.py keeps the original
body, tummy, arms, feet, eyes, beak and nostril contours, removes all shadow
and highlight layers, and uses solid charcoal, white and yellow fills.
The resulting tux-standard-flat-color.svg contains no gradients or filters.
The menu retains the original Tux proportions and colored beak and feet.
